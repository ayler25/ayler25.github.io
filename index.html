Welcome

Hi, I’m Ayla. Welcome to Relief Yoga with Ayla.

My goal is to create a welcoming space for gentle, accessible yoga and mindful movement. Whether you’re looking to slow down, reconnect with your body, build mobility, or simply take some time for yourself, you’re welcome here.

Based in Sparks, Nevada, I’ll be sharing yoga classes, movement practices, and online resources designed to help you move and feel better in your everyday life.

More classes, videos, and online options are coming soon.

What I Offer

Recovery & Renewal

A supportive space for people in recovery from addiction who are looking to reconnect with themselves through yoga, mindful movement, and self-care practices.

Spiritual Connection

Practices that create space to slow down, turn inward, strengthen your spirituality, and connect with what feels meaningful to you.

Mobility & Mindful Movement

Gentle, accessible movement for anyone seeking greater mobility, body awareness, and a more comfortable relationship with movement.

Mindful Self-Care

A place to root into simple, sustainable practices of breath, movement, presence, and compassionate self-care.
