Welcome

Welcome to Yoga Relief with Ayla.

I’m here to guide you toward finding relief through gentle, accessible yoga and mindful movement. I believe yoga should be accessible to every body, creating space for recovery, mobility, and overall well-being.

Whether you’re looking to slow down, reconnect with your body, build mobility, or simply take some time for yourself, you are welcome here.

Based in Sparks, Nevada, I offer yoga practices through Zoom and YouTube, along with resources designed to meet you where you are and help you move, breathe, and feel better in everyday life.

No forcing. No fixing.
Just finding relief.
Let’s see what you are capable of.

What I Offer

Recovery & Renewal

A supportive space to reconnect with yourself through yoga, mindful movement, and self-care during recovery from addiction.

Spiritual Connection

Slow down, turn inward, and create space for reflection, connecting spiritually, and discovering what feels meaningful to you.

Mobility & Mindful Movement

Gentle, accessible movement that can be practiced seated or standing, supporting mobility, body awareness, and greater comfort in your body.

Mindful Self-Care

Simple, sustainable practices of breath, movement, and presence that encourage compassion, acceptance, and self-love.
